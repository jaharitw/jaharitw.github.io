Animal Life Uploader

A personal tool that publishes animated animal life-story videos to its owner's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in once with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves posting.
  2. Each day the tool creates an original AI-animated video telling one animal's life story, with narration and captions.
  3. It uploads the video to the owner's own TikTok account using the Content Posting API, labelled as AI-generated content.

Who can use it

Only the owner of this tool. It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not post to anyone else's account.
