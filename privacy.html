Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

What we collect

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the tool receives from TikTok: the account's basic profile information (display name, username, avatar) and an access token that allows it to post videos to that account.

How it is used

This information is used only to confirm which account is connected and to upload videos to it. The tool does not read, collect or store any other TikTok users' data.

Storage

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never sold, shared or sent to any third party other than TikTok.

Deleting your data

Disconnect the app in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions, and delete the tool's saved token file. The tool then holds no data about you.
